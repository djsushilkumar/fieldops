# 📱 FieldOps Android APK Distribution

This folder contains the pre-compiled Android Application Package (APK) for the **FieldOps** Enterprise Field Force & Task Management platform.

---

## 📦 APK Specifications
* **File Name:** `fieldops-app-debug.apk`
* **Size:** ~90 MB
* **Target Platforms:** Android 6.0 (API 23) to Android 15 (API 35)
* **Architectures Supported:** Universal (ARM64-v8a, ARMv7, x86_64)
* **Pre-bundled Modules:**
  * Permanent Journey Plan (PJP) & Route Optimization
  * Field Sales CRM & Secondary SKU Order Booking
  * Conveyance & Odometer Mileage Claims
  * Smart Face Biometric Attendance
  * Interactive Task Kanban Board

---

## 📲 Installation Instructions

1. **Download APK**:
   Click on [`fieldops-app-debug.apk`](fieldops-app-debug.apk) and click **Download Raw** (or transfer it to your phone via USB / Google Drive / Telegram).
2. **Enable Unknown Sources**:
   On your Android phone, go to **Settings** ➔ **Security** ➔ **Install Unknown Apps** (or allow your browser / file manager).
3. **Install & Launch**:
   Tap the downloaded `fieldops-app-debug.apk` file and tap **Install**. Open **FieldOps** and log in or start exploring.
